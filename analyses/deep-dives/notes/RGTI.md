### Business in one paragraph

Rigetti Computing builds superconducting quantum processors, quantum cloud services and on-premise systems such as Novera. It controls chip design, fabrication and system integration, which can speed iteration but requires heavy capital and specialized talent. Revenue is mainly research contracts, system sales and cloud access, still far below operating expenses. The investment case hinges on whether Rigetti can improve fidelity and scale multi-chip systems fast enough to win government, academic and enterprise budgets before cash burn, dilution or larger competitors narrow its window.

### Bull case vs bear case

- **Bull:** Ankaa-3 fidelity proves durable, the chiplet roadmap scales, and government or lab customers buy systems while cloud usage grows. With cash runway improved, Rigetti can demonstrate progress without near-term balance-sheet stress.
- **Bear:** Larger rivals set the pace, Rigetti misses fidelity or qubit milestones, revenue remains lumpy, and equity markets become less willing to fund losses. A higher cash balance helps, but dilution risk remains if commercialization stays distant.

### Catalysts to watch (next 6–12 months)

1. Delivery and performance data for the 100-plus qubit roadmap milestone.
2. Novera orders, government contracts and any repeat customers.
3. Cash burn versus reported cash and investment balances.
4. Technical metrics: gate fidelity, uptime, calibration time and multi-chip performance.
5. Competitive announcements from IBM, Google, IonQ and neutral-atom peers.

### What would change the verdict

- **More constructive:** verified fidelity gains at larger scale, recurring contract revenue, controlled burn and no need for new equity.
- **More cautious:** missed roadmap dates, falling cash from faster burn, weak bookings, or a competitor demonstrating materially better error-corrected progress.
