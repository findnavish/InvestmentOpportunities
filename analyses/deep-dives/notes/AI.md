### Business in one paragraph

C3.ai sells enterprise AI applications, a development platform and generative-AI tools for industries such as energy, manufacturing, defense, financial services, utilities and state/local government. Revenue is mostly subscription and consumption-oriented software plus professional services, supported by go-to-market alliances with Microsoft, AWS, Google Cloud, Baker Hughes and systems integrators. The company has meaningful cash, but it is still loss-making and must prove that pilot activity converts into durable production contracts. The investment case hinges on whether generative AI demand revives growth while management narrows losses without overusing stock compensation or diluting shareholders.

### Bull case vs bear case

- **Bull:** Generative AI pulls more customers from pilots to production, Microsoft and Baker Hughes drive larger deals, and operating discipline narrows losses while cash remains ample. C3.ai then becomes a specialized enterprise AI applications vendor rather than a perpetual proof-of-concept business.
- **Bear:** Revenue growth slows as customers consolidate on hyperscaler tools, services needs keep margins from scaling, and profitability targets slip. Cash runway helps, but dilution and stock compensation can still limit per-share upside if losses persist.

### Catalysts to watch (next 6–12 months)

1. Quarterly bookings, remaining performance obligations and pilot-to-production conversion.
2. Operating-loss trajectory and cash burn after any restructuring or sales changes.
3. Microsoft, Baker Hughes and federal deal activity.
4. Customer concentration and churn indicators.
5. Evidence that generative-AI products carry better margins than services-heavy deployments.

### What would change the verdict

- **More constructive:** accelerating subscription growth, larger production deployments, lower sales friction through partners and a credible route to breakeven without dilution.
- **More cautious:** guide-downs, management turnover, rising services mix, persistent cash burn or signs that hyperscaler-native AI tools are displacing C3.ai projects.
