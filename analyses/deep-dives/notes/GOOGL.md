### Business in one paragraph

Alphabet is a collection of scaled internet and AI platforms, with Google Search, YouTube, Android, Chrome, Google Play and advertising tools funding Google Cloud, Gemini, Waymo and other bets. Search advertising remains the profit engine; YouTube and Cloud add growth, while TPUs, Gemini and developer tools are meant to defend Google's relevance as AI changes how people discover information. The investment case hinges on whether AI expands Google Cloud and improves Search monetization faster than it cannibalizes classic links, and whether antitrust remedies stop short of weakening the distribution advantages that made Search dominant.

### Bull case vs bear case

- **Bull:** Gemini improves Search rather than replaces it, Cloud keeps scaling with TPU differentiation, and antitrust remedies remain manageable. YouTube monetization and Waymo optionality add growth beyond the core ad engine.
- **Bear:** AI shifts queries away from Google's highest-margin formats, regulators weaken default distribution, and Cloud fails to close the gap with larger rivals. Higher capex would then dilute the margin story without enough new revenue.

### What would change the verdict

- **More constructive:** Search growth holding after AI Overviews, Cloud margins expanding, and remedies proving operationally manageable.
- **More cautious:** query share erosion, rising traffic-acquisition or inference costs, or antitrust outcomes that weaken Android and Chrome distribution.
